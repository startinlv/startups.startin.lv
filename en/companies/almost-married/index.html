Almost Married

https://almostmarried.app/

Dating platform that replaces endless swiping with a 24-hour symbolic digital marriage. AI introduces one compatible person at a time, and if both agree, they enter an exclusive 24-hour experience designed to encourage meaningful conversations, shared activities, and genuine human connection. The goal is simple: less choice, more commitment, and better relationships.
