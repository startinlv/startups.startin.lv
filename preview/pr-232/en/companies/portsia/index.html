Portsia

https://www.portsia.com/

Portsia is a Latvian food-tech platform connecting consumers with local restaurants to purchase unsold food at discounted prices, helping reduce food waste. The service is accessible via a mobile app, facilitating secure payments through card, Google Pay, or Apple Pay. Restaurants can list surplus meals, offering consumers affordable, fresh options while supporting sustainability efforts. The business model is B2C, promoting eco-conscious dining choices.
