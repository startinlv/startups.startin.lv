Livord is a fintech infrastructure provider specializing in payment card platforms, 3D Secure authentication, fraud prevention, and AML/CTF automation. They serve banks, PSPs, and processors in the MEA region, offering modular, EMVCo-certified solutions for both issuing and acquiring. Their business model is B2B, focusing on compliance and scalability.
